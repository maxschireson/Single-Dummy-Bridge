# Pre-7 test data set (12 November 2026)

Played results of the Gen 7 standard candidate against Gen 6 fast, collected on four test machines
while the arena was still on Gen 6. **Finished: 1,637 boards, stopped at 02:52 to 02:58 UTC when
phase 1 of Gen 7 was authorized and the machines became arena collectors.** Max asked for the data
to be kept on GitHub. It was played as a test run, not as an arena cohort, and nothing here
authorizes Gen 7.

**On the dashboard.** Max decided on 12 November (03:09 UTC) that these boards can be added to the
dashboard with the Gen 7 results, with no need to distinguish the small difference in settings
there (trust 0.25 here, 0.3 in the arena). Another session exported them as frozen feeds,
`arena-gen7-pre7-azure1-feed` to `arena-gen7-pre7-azure4-feed`, from the transfer branches below.
Analyses that compare trust settings should still keep the two apart: the manifests record which
is which.

## Where

One `transfer/` branch per machine, each written only by that machine
([ops/publish_test_run.py](../ops/publish_test_run.py), every ten minutes):

| Branch | Run folder | Block of deals (positions in the source's unplayed list) | Boards played |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `transfer/pre7-test-azure1-20261112` | `g7s-vs-g6f-01` | 0 to 5,599 | 538 |
| `transfer/pre7-test-azure2-20261112` | `pre7-7s-vs-6f-azure2` | 5,627 to 11,253 | 363 |
| `transfer/pre7-test-azure3-20261112` | `pre7-7s-vs-6f-azure3` | 11,254 to 16,880 | 374 |
| `transfer/pre7-test-azure4-20261112` | `pre7-7s-vs-6f-azure4` | 16,881 to 22,507 | 362 |

Each run played the first boards of its block in order (a few of the last indices are missing where
boards were still in flight at the stop), so the played deals are the start of each block.

A run folder holds `manifest.json` (every bot's full settings and hashes), `plan.json`,
`state.json`, `source-indices.json`, `corpus.jsonl`, `episodes/NNNNNN.json.gz`,
`receipts/NNNNNN.json` and `failures/`. Episodes are in the feeds' format, so
`scripts/gen7_alpha_report.py --episodes FOLDER`, `scripts/gen7_match_forks.py` and
`scripts/gen7_build_dataset.py` read them as they are. The DDS cache and the run's copied model
files are not published; the manifest has their hashes and the models are in `models/`.

## What was played

- **Pair:** `7s-32~6-32`, both rooms of every deal, common bidder `6-32` (the two bots bid alike,
  so the common and native results are the same).
- **`7s-32`:** the candidate of 11 November 23:30 UTC, exactly as in
  [configs/round7-candidate-phase1.json](../configs/round7-candidate-phase1.json): policy v4,
  `play_weight` 0.5, net tie-break, and the tree at tricks 3 to 6 with `root_margin` 0.25, `trust`
  0.25, the stake rule at `path` 0.5, 3 tricks, 3 cards a node, `mass` 0.98, 8 layouts, `plain` 1.
  The test plan differs from that file only in `launch_enabled`, `pending`, the roster and pair
  weights (this pair alone), the audit references kept for the roster, and the title.
- **Deals:** the 22,508 unplayed deals of the `arena-gen6-gen7-capture-feed` recovery capsule at
  `8833c62`, in its order and with its seed. Each machine has its own block, so no deal is played
  twice. Blocks come from `ops/prepare_gen7_alpha.py --first N --count N`, added on this branch.
- **Forks:** carried from that run, as preparation always does: tie audits at probability 0.5, gap
  forks at 2 a band with bands at 0.125 and 0.5, and the tree's own audits (where the tree changed
  the card, the fork plays the card without the tree). A board is two root tables and about twelve
  fork tables.
- **Code:** `azure1` runs `feature/gen7-candidate-20261111` at `1042db5`. The others run this
  branch, which changes `ops/prepare_gen7_alpha.py` and adds the publisher; `bridge_harness/`,
  `scripts/` and `native/` are the same files, and each manifest pins their hash.

## Result

All four machines, 1,637 boards, no failures: `7s-32` +0.25 +- 0.08 IMPs a board (265 won, 157 lost,
1,215 flat), 23,219 tables. From the receipts; `scripts/gen7_alpha_report.py` on the four episode
folders gives the tree's use and its audit forks, and has not been run on the whole set.

### First 308 boards (azure1, 02:03 UTC)

No failures. `7s-32` +0.38 +- 0.16 IMPs a board (51 won, 28 lost, 229 flat). The tree was
consulted at 47% of decisions at tricks 3 to 6 and moved the card at 2.9%; its 325 audit forks came
to -0.15 +- 0.18 IMPs a fork (tricks 3-4 +0.04 +- 0.26, tricks 5-6 -0.30 +- 0.18). 959 boards an
hour on 8 cores with 8 workers.

## Limits

- A match of one configuration against one opponent. It says nothing about BEN or older bots.
- The deals come from one capsule, the one the earlier alpha matches drew their first deals from,
  so `azure1`'s first boards are not independent of those matches.
- Whether these deals are disjoint from the deals policy v4 was trained on was not checked.
- `azure1`'s first 14 boards were played before the workers were held to one thread each. Play is
  unaffected; their decision timings are slow.
- Each machine's worker count is in its branch's README; timings are not comparable across
  machines with different counts.
- `ops/publish_test_run.py` and the `--first`/`--count` options were tested on these runs and by
  `tests/test_gen7_launch.py`; they have not been used for an arena cohort. For arena cohorts use
  `--shard K/N` on `feature/gen7-candidate-20261111` (`fe382f9` and later), which the phase 1
  collectors on these machines use; this branch was cut before it and does not have it.
- Phase 1 cohorts draw on the same capsule by shard, so some of their deals are deals played here.
  The bots differ (trust 0.3 in the arena, 0.25 here), so the arena does not count them twice, but
  the two sets of results are not independent.
- One machine ran 10 workers for its 374 boards; it was not measurably faster than the two with 8
  (149 boards against 133 and 152 in the first nine minutes).
