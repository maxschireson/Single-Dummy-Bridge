## Hypothesis

On the current ladder cells, the order of the top five members does not depend on the field, and the Nash average is too noisy to rank by. Predictions, not known values: the top-five order is the same under equal weights over the population and under a field of BEN 64, Gen 8, Gen 7.1 and Gen 6 at equal shares; the maximum-entropy equilibrium puts its weight on three members or fewer; the bootstrap standard errors of the Nash-average scores are at least twice those of the field scores.

## Mechanism

Equal weights over a population let redundant members define strength; the Nash average does not (Balduzzi et al. 2018), but it rests on the cells nearest the top, which are the noisiest (docs/gen9/arena-selfplay.md, section 5.2).

## Primary metric and population

Rank order and Spearman correlation of the field scores under the two fields; support of the equilibrium; bootstrap (by board within pair) standard errors. The ladder's cells (64,933 native boards from 111 feeds at its last refresh).

## Regression slices

With and without the bench priors; native and common conditions where both exist.

## Compute cap

30 minutes in the sandbox.

## Stopping rule

One pass.

## Result

Evidence: on the ladder's 22 points (67,582 native boards; the Gen 9 cohort's 92 boards left out) the top-five order is the same under equal weights over the population and under the example field BEN 64, 8s-32, 71s-32, 6-32 (Spearman 0.999 over all 22). The maximum-entropy equilibrium is 8s-32 alone in the point estimate and in all 200 bootstrap replicates, so each point's Nash average is its cell against 8s-32; it swaps BEN 64 and 6-256 (fourth and fifth, within one SE). The Nash averages' bootstrap SEs are about those of the field scores (median ratio 1.09 with priors, 1.17 without), not twice. t2 = 0.0067: the population is close to transitive. Predictions (1) met, (2) met, (3) not met. Report: reports/gen9-arena-field-2026-11-13 (feature/gen9 83c00df).
Sample: 67,582 native boards from 112 feeds (rows collected 07:14 UTC), 200 bootstrap replicates by board within pair, with and without bench priors (same conclusions).
Revisit if: the population gains cycles or several strong styles (synthetic styles admitted in numbers, exploiters, guests), when the field choice and the Nash average can disagree with equal weights.
