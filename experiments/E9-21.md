## Hypothesis

On the current ladder cells, the order of the top five members does not depend on the field, and the Nash average is too noisy to rank by. Predictions, not known values: the top-five order is the same under equal weights over the population and under a field of BEN 64, Gen 8, Gen 7.1 and Gen 6 at equal shares; the maximum-entropy equilibrium puts its weight on three members or fewer; the bootstrap standard errors of the Nash-average scores are at least twice those of the field scores.

## Mechanism

Equal weights over a population let redundant members define strength; the Nash average does not (Balduzzi et al. 2018), but it rests on the cells nearest the top, which are the noisiest (docs/gen9/arena-selfplay.md, section 5.2).

## Primary metric and population

Rank order and Spearman correlation of the field scores under the two fields; support of the equilibrium; bootstrap (by board within pair) standard errors. The ladder's cells (64,933 native boards from 111 feeds at its last refresh).

## Regression slices

With and without the bench priors; native and common conditions where both exist.
