## Hypothesis

At a conditional-claim threshold of 0.9, the layout weights decide which lines are committed, and against a system the house scorer was not trained on, the scorer's weights commit lines that fail far more often than Gen 9's (E9-24's post-hoc result on Precision defenders, registered here as a prediction on a new sample as will recommended at 09:21 UTC). Predictions, not known values, on native tables where BEN defends (BEN 64 and BEN 16 in the Gen 8 BEN feeds), first declarer-side decisions at 8 and 7 cards, no outright claim: (1) lines committed at 0.9 or more fail at least 1.5 times as often with the scorer's weights as with Gen 9's; (2) among positions where only one weighting commits, the scorer-only lines cover the actual layout less often than the Gen 9-only lines (exact McNemar on the discordant pairs, p below 0.05); (3) Gen 9's failure rate is within 3 points of the house-defended control from the same feeds.

## Mechanism

A cell's weight is the summed weight of its layouts. The scorer reads every auction as a house auction; Gen 9 reads BEN's calls by replaying BEN's own bidder (bridge_harness/cards/replay.py, ReplayReader on the BEN 64 bidding net the arena's BEN lanes use).

## Primary metric and population

Failure rate of committed lines (the actual layout's cell outside the chosen card's certified set) at floors of 0.9 or more, by weighting; discordant pairs as in E9-24. Positions: native tables of the Gen 8 BEN feeds (arena-gen8-*-ben64-* and BEN 16 feeds) where both defenders are BEN, shape level; control: the same feeds' house-defended positions.

## Regression slices

8 against 7 cards; games, slams and doubled against partscores.

## Compute cap

Shape level, at most 1,500 positions per population, 6 CPU-hours in the sandbox.

## Stopping rule

One pass over the stated positions.

## Result

Not confirmed as registered. Report: `feature/gen9:reports/gen9-e9-25-2026-11-14` (4724cd7d); box run 14 Nov 09:08 to 09:15 UTC at `adae5fbb`, results on `transfer/gen9-e9-25-azure4-20261114` (c3033825). With BEN 64 defending, committed lines failed 8 of 160 times with the scorer's weights (5.0% +- 1.7) and 5 of 165 with Gen 9's (3.0% +- 1.3).

- (1) Ratio 1.65, 95% interval 0.55 to 4.94: met on the point estimate only.
- (2) Scorer-only lines covered 4 of 6, Gen 9-only lines 10 of 11, Fisher one-sided p = 0.27; where both commit (154), only Gen 9 covers 3 and only the scorer 1, McNemar exact p = 0.625: not met.
- (3) Gen 9 3.0% against the house-defended control's 10.0% (10 of 100), -7.0 +- 3.3 points: not met. The control is the other side of the same tables (BEN declares, `8s-32` defends), so it differs in declarers and contracts too.
- Slices: the difference is in games, slams and doubled contracts (4 of 74 against 1 of 76); partscores 4 of 86 against 4 of 89.
- Deviations: only `arena-gen8-azure3-ben64-1` to `-8` were read (no Gen 8 BEN 16 feeds exist; the `-f-` and `-p2-` families were not read; the 1,500 cap was reached from the first family); (2) is reported both ways.
- Changes no play setting: conditional claims are off by default in X9 (G9-A11).

Evidence: played boards (declarer positions from the Gen 8 BEN 64 feeds, each committed line checked against the actual layout); no IMPs.
Sample: 835 BEN-defended and 742 house-defended positions without an outright claim; 160 (scorer) and 165 (Gen 9) committed lines with BEN defending; failure ratio 1.65, 95% interval 0.55 to 4.94.
