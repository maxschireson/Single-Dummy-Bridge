## Hypothesis

Registered after the measurement (G9-S6 is descriptive; no prediction was written first). Question: do the house bots' spot cards and opening leads follow any declared scheme, and how strongly?

## Mechanism

House bots choose among tied cards by the policy net's habit (Gen 7: `tie_break: net`; Gen 6: the Gen 5 net). Nothing in their training rewards a spot that tells partner a fact, so any signal would be an accident of the net.

## Primary metric and population

Per bot and signal: P(high spot | first message) - P(high spot | second message) over signalling occasions (two or more spots of the class held), with its standard error; for opening leads, the share of leads that are each convention's card, against chance (mean of 1 / cards held). Population: `arena-gen7-phase1-azure1-feed` at `f9dad79`, 828 episodes, 1,824 main tables, house defenders 7s-32, 6-32, 5RZ32, 1. Kind of evidence: exact counts after the board.

## Regression slices

The class cut to the bot's own tie set (what its tie-break chose among) against the whole class held.

## Compute cap

Minutes in a sandbox.

## Stopping rule

The fixed sample.

## Result

**No agreed spot signals; a fourth-best lead habit.**
- Spots: every association is within ±0.12; the largest for 7s-32 is its first discard, +0.08 ± 0.03 toward "high from even" (count). One of 36 bot-and-signal cells is beyond two standard errors otherwise (bot 1, first discard, -0.11 ± 0.05). The spot played is the lowest of the class about 60% of the time, the highest about 25%, a middle one about 15%.
- Opening leads: fourth best 0.48 (7s-32, 853 leads), 0.45 (6-32), 0.41 (5RZ32), 0.39 (1), against 0.30 by chance; ace from AK 167 of 179; top of touching honours 192 of 295.
- Tie-set cut: hit rates within 0.04 of the whole class for spots, 0.09 for leads.
- Draft house carding descriptor and note: `reports/gen9-house-carding-2026-11-12/README.md` (fourth-best leads, ace from AK, top of sequences, no spot signals).
- Outcome: **adopted** as the house bots' measured carding (for disclosure and for other players' readers; the house reader keeps the net).
- Data and code: `feature/gen9-signals` at `b301f7b`: `scripts/gen9_house_carding.py`, `scripts/gen9_house_signal_association.py`, `reports/gen9-house-carding-2026-11-12/`.
