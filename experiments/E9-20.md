## Hypothesis

The rated population differs mostly in card play engine and hardly in system or carding, and several ladder members are redundant by both results and behaviour. Predictions, not known values: at least three pairs of ladder members have results rows within noise of each other; the declared distance between any two house bots is below 5% of the card descriptor's fields.

## Mechanism

The house lineage shares one bidding family and signals nothing (E9-9); variants were added for experiments, not for variety (docs/gen9/arena-selfplay.md, section 3).

## Primary metric and population

For each pair of ladder members (gh-pages arena/ladder.json, 22 points): results distance (rows of the cells matrix, in standard errors), declared distance (share of the 81 descriptor fields that differ), behaviour distance (share of differing top choices on a 1,000-decision subset of p41's pools for the ten most played members). The list of redundant pairs under the rule of section 3.3 at thresholds of 3%, 5% and 10%.

## Regression slices

Card play decisions with a choice; opening leads; tricks 1 to 6.

## Compute cap

3 CPU-hours in the sandbox.

## Stopping rule

One pass.

## Result

Evidence: (1) results: 19 pairs of ladder points have results rows within noise and strengths within 0.1, including 6-32 with 8c-32 (house system against Precision) and 6-256 with BEN 64; with t2 = 0.0067 the results row is little more than the strength. (2) behaviour, from 141,234 paired card decisions on common-auction boards (same hands, same auction, bots in swapped seats, up to the first divergence, forced cards out): 8c-32 and 8s-32 never chose differently in 25,953 decisions (same card play, different bidder; 8c-32 is 0.79 IMPs a board weaker natively, all bidding); 71s-32 and 8s-32 differ on 9% of cards and no opening lead; no pair of different bots is under 5% (closest: Gen 5 variants at 6.8%); house against BEN 54% to 56%; Gen 1 against the rest 74% to 78%. (3) declared: one bidding family for every house bot and no carding; only 8c-32 and BEN differ. Prediction (1) met; prediction (2) not tested field by field (the house bots declare one family); the hypothesis that several members are redundant on both results and behaviour is not met at a 5% card-level threshold: no current member is redundant. Design consequence: the rule needs all three distances, and the behaviour threshold must count choices that differ in value, since randomizing among equal cards makes cards differ. Report: reports/gen9-arena-diversity-2026-11-13 (feature/gen9 6a6c533).
Sample: 22 ladder points, 67,582 native boards (results); 141,234 paired decisions from up to five board shards per feed of 113 registered feeds (behaviour).
Revisit if: randomizing bots or synthetic styles join (recompute behaviour on value-changing choices), or a variant is proposed whose only change is a setting the paired decisions can test.
