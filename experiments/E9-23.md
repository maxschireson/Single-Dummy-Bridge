## Hypothesis

A bot that mixes among equal cards by the net's probabilities leaks less to an informed reader. Prediction, not a known value: an informed reader gains at most 0.10 tricks a board from `8x3-32`'s cards against about 0.12 from `8s-32`'s (`8p-32`: 0.124 +- 0.026, E9-16).

## Mechanism

E9-16 found most of what a reader of our strength learns comes from which of several equal cards we chose; 8X v2 and v3 mix among equal cards (x8, commits 0bcdbc2 and 1a779a9).

## Primary metric and population

E9-16's leak measurement (reports/gen9-leakage-2026-11-12) with each bot's own policy as the reader's model, in double-dummy tricks a board on p41's house pools.

## Regression slices

Declarer and defenders; tricks 1 to 6.

## Compute cap

4 CPU-hours in the sandbox; after the review package unless time allows.

## Stopping rule

One pass per bot.
