## Hypothesis

Moving the bot's belief toward the true deal by `g` nats (the gain in the true layout's log weight over the pool the readers weigh, at every decision after the opening lead) raises its result against the same bot unhelped, roughly in proportion for small `g`, at about 0.3 to 0.4 IMPs a board per nat, flattening toward the bound. The one point we have: Gen 8's reading from trick 1 raised the true layout's weight by about 1.1 nats over tricks 1 to 6 and won 0.40 IMPs a board against Gen 7 (board entries of 03:14 and 06:48 UTC, 12 Nov). The bound itself: a bot that knows the deal. Predictions, not known values.

## Mechanism

The search plays the double-dummy best card on its weighted layouts. Weight moved toward the truth moves its choices toward the best card on the actual deal. This is the exchange rate for every reader in Gen 9: a reader is measured in nats offline, and this curve says what those nats buy.

## Primary metric and population

IMPs a board of a helped `8p-32` against `8p-32`, fork-free, fresh deals, 800 boards a setting, and the realised gain per decision recorded with each decision (the x axis). **Test-only bots: they are given the deal on purpose and carry an `oracle` key that arena preparation must refuse** (`configs/gen9-proto-bots.json`, `scripts/gen9_match.py`, `BRIDGE_ALLOW_ORACLE=1`).

Two forms of help, both in `bridge_harness/gen9.py`:

- **Primary, `near`** (bots `9n005-32`, `9n01-32`, `9n02-32`, `9n04-32`): every layout of the pool is multiplied by `exp(-near * d)`, `d` = the cards it places in the wrong hidden hand, counted by rank. Belief moves toward layouts like the true one and the true one is never handed over, which is what a reader does. The gain is `-log(mean over the pool of exp(-near * d))`.
- **Secondary, `tilt`** (bots `9t05-32` to `9t4-32`, `9tinf-32`): the exact true layout is put among the layouts the search solves, at `exp(tilt)` times their mean weight; `inf` is double dummy on the true deal (the bound). Before the deal list the 32 solved layouts almost never hold the true one (0% to 30% at tricks 1 to 6 in the pilot), so this form hands over something no reader would.

## Regression slices

Help only as declarer; only as defender. Help only at tricks 1 to 6; only from trick 7 (bots `9t1d`, `9t1f`, `9t1e`, `9t1l` for the secondary form; the same split for `near` at one setting).
