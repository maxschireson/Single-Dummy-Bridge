## Hypothesis

On auctions bid with a system the hand-fit scorer was not trained on, the generic scorer plus the rule filter compiled from that system's card recovers at least half of the gap, in the true layout's weight, between the generic scorer alone and an exact reading of the generator. On house auctions read with a card that describes the house system it changes the weight by less than the noise. Played out (part 2), a bot reading with the filter beats the same bot without it when all four seats bid the foreign system.

These thresholds are predictions, not known values.

## Mechanism

The scorer reads every call as the house system would mean it. The card removes hands that the call cannot hold under the declared meaning, which matters most for calls whose meaning differs (strong club, limited openings, a different no-trump range).

## Primary metric and population

**Part 1 (prediction; no solver).** Gain in log weight of the true layout: `G(R) = log(w_R(true) / sum over the pool of w_R) - log(1 / n)`, for a pool of `n = 512` layouts drawn from the prior that fit the decider's view, the true one included. Deciders: the opening leader before the lead, and declarer at trick 1. Readers `R`: generic scorer (hand-fit v0); generic plus filter (`eps` 0.05), with and without negative inference; the filter alone at `eps` 0.001 with negative inference (the ceiling: the generator is deterministic); and, if trained, a Gen 8 system expert for that system.

Systems: the rule bidder's precision and standard settings and three sampled variants (for example a 12 to 14 no-trump, lighter openings, no transfers). 2,000 random deals a system, all four seats bidding it; plus one mixed table type (house bidder against precision).

**Part 2 (played boards; needs a box).** `scripts/gen8_match.py`, the rule system as the common bidder at all four seats, the Gen 8 prototype bot with the filter against the same bot without. IMPs a board with its standard error.

## Regression slices

Leader and declarer separately. Auctions where every call is covered by a table, against auctions that reach the general placing procedure. Contested and uncontested auctions. House auctions with a house-like card (expected: no change).

## Compute cap

Part 1: 2 CPU-hours in a sandbox. Part 2: one box for 2 hours.

## Stopping rule

Part 1: the fixed samples above. Part 2: 1,600 boards for the precision setting, then stop; more only if g9 asks.
