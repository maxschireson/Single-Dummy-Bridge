## Goal

Max, 14:45 UTC, to g8: 'We should have a card-reader-by-convention card... Some version for 9!' The version exists (sam's reader from a declared carding: signals.py, gen9_play.py; it is playing in the arrival run). This is its cleanest test, on g8's stand-in carders in configs/gen8-proto-bots.json: t-high and t-low play the highest or lowest of their equal cards, t-random a random one. Hypothesis: a reader told the rule (an emission table that puts the card at the top, or the bottom, of its class whatever the hand's count; for t-random an even choice) beats a reader that only scales the net (8x-32: factors 0.2, 0.5, 0.25) and one that reads the net in full (8p-32, which g8 measured at -0.36 +- 0.13 IMPs against t-high relative to the scaled reader). Prediction: against t-high and t-low the rule reader gains +0.1 IMPs a board or more over 8x-32; against t-random it equals 8x-32 (there is nothing in the card to read) and both beat 8p-32.
