## Hypothesis

Against BEN, a Gen 9 bot that carries a model of BEN (its measured play factor and floor by role and trick, its lead conventions and signal habits with measured honesty, and, if E9-8 supports it, its calls read by replaying BEN's own bidder) wins more IMPs a board than the Gen 8 prototype does against BEN on the same deals. Gen 7 against BEN was level (+0.09 +- 0.12 at 16 samples, +0.07 +- 0.15 at 64; board, 06:12 UTC 12 Nov), and the prototype reads an unknown opponent's cards at factor 0.3. Prediction: a difference of +0.2 IMPs a board or more. Not a known value.

## Mechanism

BEN is the one outside player in the arena and there are about 2,000 recorded boards against it. Everything Gen 9 adds for a foreign player can be built for BEN from those boards (E9-7) and then tested where it counts, on fresh boards against BEN itself.

## Primary metric and population

Played boards, fork-free, fresh deals not in any net's training families: `9b-32~ben` and `8p-32~ben` on the same deals; the difference in IMPs a board with its standard error. At least 1,600 boards each at BEN (16). BEN's servers as on g7's lanes (one server per worker).

## Regression slices

BEN (64). Declarer play against defence (where does the gain come from). The same bots against `8p-32`, where the BEN model must change nothing.

## Compute cap

One box with BEN servers for 8 hours.

## Stopping rule

The fixed samples; or the promotion test of `docs/gen9/arena-statistics.md` once dave's runner can apply it.
