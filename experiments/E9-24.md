## Hypothesis

The conditional claims' certified probability is only as good as the cells' weights, and the hand-fit scorer's weights are worst where the opponents bid another system. Predictions, not known values: on native tables where the defending pair bids Precision (8c-32), the scorer overstates the certified probability above 0.25 by more than it does on house-only tables (the study: 4 to 15 points); Gen 9's weights for that pair (the rb-precision profile through the replay reader) halve the overstatement there; on house-only tables Gen 9's weights and the scorer's give the same table within its errors.

## Mechanism

A cell's weight is the summed weight of its layouts (DESIGN.md section 4). The scorer reads every auction as a house auction; Gen 9's mixture reads a declared or recognized system as that system (architecture section 4).

## Primary metric and population

The "claimed against realised" table of cells/README.md section 4 (mean floor of the best card against the share of positions whose actual layout is inside its certified set, by floor band) for three weight sources: scorer; 8X read_layouts (scorer plus the net's reading of the play); Gen 9. Positions: the first declarer-side decision with 8 cards and no outright claim, native tables of the Gen 8 feeds where 8c-32 defends; control: house-only tables of the same feeds.

## Regression slices

7 cards; games, slams and doubled contracts against partscores; competitive auctions.

## Compute cap

The shape level only, at most 1,000 positions per population, 6 CPU-hours in the sandbox.

## Stopping rule

One pass per weight source.

## Result

Evidence: native tables of eight Gen 8 feeds seating 8c-32; first declarer-side decisions at 8 and 7 cards, no outright claim. The scorer gives the actual layout under 1% of a uniform weight in 108 of 667 Precision-defended positions at 8 cards and 86 of 563 at 7; Gen 9's replay of 8c-32's own bidder (identity f50e95de checked) in none. Floor minus realised above 0.25: house control +0.060 +- 0.025 (8) and +0.049 +- 0.029 (7); Precision defends, scorer +0.099 +- 0.024 and +0.107 +- 0.029; Gen 9 +0.083 +- 0.023 and +0.089 +- 0.027; two named cards (8) +0.089 against +0.096. Lines committed at a floor of 0.9 or more (7 and 8 cards): scorer 231, covered 0.913 +- 0.019; Gen 9 228, covered 0.965 +- 0.012; house control 180, 0.950 +- 0.016. Both commit in 206 and cover the same 200; scorer alone 25, covered 11; Gen 9 alone 22, covered 20. Paired coverage of the best line over all positions: -0.004 +- 0.005 (8), +0.009 +- 0.007 (7), -0.022 +- 0.013 (two named). Predictions: (1) direction as predicted, each under 1.5 SE, not shown; (2) not met (a sixth less, not half); (3) met by construction. 8X's read_layouts weighting not run. Report: reports/gen9-sid-weights-2026-11-13 (feature/gen9 33ddcbe).
Sample: 2,457 Precision-defended and 2,247 control positions (shape level, every position); 620 Precision-defended 8-card positions with two named cards; one opponent system.
Revisit if: a guest whose bidder Gen 9 does not hold (card or scorer reading only), the play reading is added to either weighting, or conditional claims are played (contracts and IMPs instead of coverage).
