## Hypothesis

For a bot v2 never saw, the row of the nearest known profile predicts its decisions better than the generic row. Predictions, not known values: on the arrival run's guests, v2 with the row chosen from the arrival mixture beats the generic row by at least 0.02 in log-loss; on a bot v2 knows, its own row beats the generic row by about plan's 0.10 (the control). Second part: the learning curve, nats a call and IMPs a board against boards seen, from the arrival run's epochs.

## Mechanism

Gen 9's arrival mixture (card to profiles) is a prior on a newcomer that the nets do not use today; v2 reads unknown names through the generic row (docs/gen9/arena-selfplay.md, section 7).

## Primary metric and population

Log-loss on the guests' card decisions with a choice: generic row, nearest-profile row, mixture of rows weighted by the arrival mixture. Records: `transfer/gen9-d6-arrival-azure4-20261112` (6,200 boards). Net: `transfer/rollout-net-v2-20261113` `runs/w768/policy_epoch5.npz`.

## Regression slices

Signalling situations (count, attitude) against the rest; tricks 1 to 6 and 7 to 13.

## Compute cap

Inference only, 2 CPU-hours in the sandbox.

## Stopping rule

One pass.
