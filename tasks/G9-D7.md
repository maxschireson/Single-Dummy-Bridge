## Goal

After every board of an epoch, compute the experience deltas for each partnership seen (bill's CardFactor.observe_board for calls, sam's signal_experience for cards), write them to the store, and at the epoch boundary freeze a snapshot and hand each bot the other side's PlayerModel through meet(opponent) and the bidders through auction_by(bidders). Bidding statistics go to whoever made the calls, card statistics to whoever played the cards.

## Done when

A two-epoch run on one machine with a carding bot at known honesty and a rule-bidder system: in epoch 2 the reader's honesty and off-card rates are the ones estimated from epoch 1's boards, the records name the snapshot, and replaying an epoch-2 board with the named snapshot reproduces it card for card. Tests.

## Context

bridge_harness/experience.py, experience_store.py, player_model.py, gen9.py (meet, auction_by) on feature/gen9. Nothing about a board may reach a snapshot before the epoch in which it was played has closed.
