## Goal

Implement `SignalModel(descriptor, honesty)` used by both sides of the table: the carding bot (which spot to play) and the reader (how likely each spot was, per layout). Add the carding bot as a player setting on the Gen 8 prototype player.

## Done when

A PR into `feature/gen9` with tests that show:
- A bot with honesty 1 plays the prescribed spot whenever it has the choice; with honesty 0 its spots carry no information.
- The reader's probabilities over a class sum to 1 in every layout.
- With no carding declared the reader's term is exactly zero and the player is the Gen 8 base.
- A bot entry for `scripts/gen8_match.py` for at least: standard count and attitude, upside-down count and attitude, each at honesty 1.0, 0.8, 0.5.
