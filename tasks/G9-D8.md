## Goal

Max (13 Nov 03:40 UTC) asked how the arena will be managed and whether the results page gets a new format, both general cleanup and reflecting the different approach. Today's page is generation-based: a hand-edited feed list (gh-pages arena/gen6-sources.json), a fixed bot order, one page per generation. Build (1) the data the page needs, written by the epoch runner at each boundary: roster (player, kind, version, declared card, status: candidate, admitted, retired), rating table (IMPs a board with interval, board count, field score, worst cell, a won/lost screen), pair table (observed mean against the model's prediction, out-of-line pairs flagged), decision log (admissions, promotions with the betting state, retirements), schedule shares by purpose, and per player what the bot has learned about it (profile probabilities, honesty, play factor); (2) a static prototype page rendered from the arrival run's records, with: roster now; ratings; pair matrix; decisions by epoch; ratings over epochs; one page per player; a tracker of each version against a fixed outside panel. Experiments stay off the arena page (they have their own files).
