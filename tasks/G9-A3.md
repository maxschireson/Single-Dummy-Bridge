## Goal

A `Gen9Player` built on the Gen 8 prototype player that takes a bidding reader (I4) and a play reader (I5) and carries the mixture over profiles through the board (architecture 4.1). This is where bill's and sam's modules plug in.

## Done when

With no card, no profile probability and empty experience it plays card for card as its Gen 8 base (tested on full tables). With two profiles it weights layouts by the log-sum-exp over profiles including the visible hands of that side (tested against a brute-force computation). A bot entry for `scripts/gen8_match.py`.
