## Goal

One run of the epoch runner on azure4 in which two players nobody has met arrive with what they declare, and everything Gen 9 is for happens in order with no code change after the run starts. This run is what Max reviews on Friday, so it comes before every other use of the box.

**Cast.**
- `9a-32`: the Gen 9 bot: the shell (`bridge_harness/gen9.py`) with the card reader (`bidding_reader`), the replay reader for house bidders (`replay_reader`), the signal reader and experience on.
- `8p-32`: its control, the same engine with no player model.
- `7s-32` and two more house bots or anchors, as the capsule allows.
- `guest-a`, submitted before epoch 2: bids the rule bidder's precision settings; plays the cards with the Gen 8 prototype engine and upside-down count and attitude at honesty 0.8 (sam's carding tie-break). Its package declares its card (`rb-precision`) and its carding descriptor, with no honesty stated. The house holds its card, not its bidder.
- `guest-b`, submitted before epoch 4: bids a house bidder (Gen 5) and declares the standard card nearest to it (`rb-standard`); plays standard signals at honesty 0 (its spots say nothing). This is the other path: a card that maps onto profiles we hold, and a declared scheme that is not followed.
- One house bot (`7s-32`) is retired before epoch 4 by a queued registry command.

**Sequence** (set on 12 Nov 11:32 UTC, before the run). Five epochs of 600, 1,200, 1,200, 1,600 and 1,600 boards (6,200). Floors on the same deals: `9a-32~guest-a` and `8p-32~guest-a` 300 boards each from epoch 2; `9a-32~guest-b` and `8p-32~guest-b` 300 each in epochs 4 and 5. The scheduler places the rest, with shares for this run only (nothing trains on it): self-play 0.10, promotion 0.30, the rest to admission and panel pairs by the gain rule. `9a-32` is activated and its test against `8p-32` opened by plan before epoch 2. Occasions for experience: bids by `auction`, cards by `play`.

**At a submission** the registry computes the newcomer's first model with `player_model.arrive`, the distances from `cards.match.card_distance` against every system we hold, and stores it with the package. **At every boundary** the experience snapshot freezes (G9-D7) and each bot is handed the other side's model with that snapshot. The shell then moves the profile probabilities by the `profile` statistic (`player_model.believed`), takes the play factor from `play.info`, and the readers take the off-card rates and the honesty from their statistics.

## Done when

On a `transfer/gen9-arrival-*` branch: the registry log, the five epoch manifests and records, the snapshots, and one page with these, each beside the prediction registered here before the run:

1. **The player model at the table.** `9a-32` against `guest-a` minus `8p-32` against `guest-a`, same deals, IMPs a board with its standard error, pooled over epochs 2 to 5 (1,200 boards a pair) and by epoch. Prediction: **+0.2 or more pooled** (lowered from +0.4 on 12 Nov 11:45 UTC, before the run, on two sandbox pilots: the card reader against the scorer on precision auctions +0.21 +- 0.13 over 400 boards, and bill's finding that a board's reading gap at the lead barely predicts its IMPs). The signals' part is not known at the table yet (E9-4 part 2).
2. **What experience adds.** By epoch: the probability on the `card` profile for `guest-a` (prediction from E9-2: above 0.95 from epoch 3), the honesty estimate for `guest-a` (prediction 0.65 to 0.8: the reader measures the effective value, sam) and for `guest-b` (truth 0; prediction: below 0.25 from epoch 5), the play factor chosen. And whether item 1's difference rises from epoch 2 to epochs 3 to 5 (prediction: by less than its standard error at this size; reported whichever way it falls).
3. **The other path.** `9a-32` against `guest-b` minus `8p-32` against `guest-b`, 600 boards a pair, by epoch. In epoch 4 the bot knows `guest-b` by its arrival prior alone (card about 0.24, the three house nets about 0.7 between them, flat 0.06); from epoch 5 by experience. Prediction: between -0.2 and +0.2 in each epoch, and pooled within one standard error of zero: the profile is one the house already reads and there is nothing in its spots to read, so the test is that being told a card and a scheme that are not followed costs nothing.
4. **The arena.** For each arrival: the registry entry, the admission decision with its width, the newcomer's schedule share by epoch, ratings with intervals by epoch, and the state of the betting test for `9a-32` against `8p-32`.
5. **Replay.** One board of epoch 3 replayed from its manifest and the named snapshot, card for card.
6. **The reading itself** (added 12 Nov, a check on the shell): by epoch and guest, the mean over the floor boards of the auction's contribution to the true layout's log weight for `9a-32` and for `8p-32`.

Before the box run: a rehearsal on one machine with the box run's events file on short epochs, its records pushed, and a note to g9. **Release check:** on the rehearsal page, item 6 shows `9a-32` not below `8p-32` by more than 0.3 nats on `guest-b` and above it on `guest-a`. g9 releases; if g9 has not answered within 30 minutes of the note, will may release on that check.
