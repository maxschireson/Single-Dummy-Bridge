## Goal

Max (13 Nov 04:30 UTC): 'Does bill have an ingestion tool to accept a convention card, either pdf or a photo of a common format (current and older ACBL, WBF)? It would be great to also be able to ingest system notes which may specify style eg only preempt good suits vul, can preempt 5 card suits etc which we would want to reflect in how we model their system.' Today the descriptor (card-descriptor-v0, 81 fields) is filled by hand or by an agent reading the card; there is no intake tool. Build: (1) fillable ACBL PDFs (the ACBL editor's cards carry form fields): read the fields directly, no OCR; (2) photos, scans, older ACBL cards and the WBF card: an intake procedure for an agent with vision (template, field-by-field instructions, provenance per field: where on the card, confidence), so that any Claude session can run it; (3) system notes in prose: a 'style' section of the descriptor for statements the rules language can express (suit quality and length by seat and vulnerability for preempts and overcalls, opening style in third seat, the HCP and shape of light openings), compiled into the rules' predicates (honour counts per suit, lengths, seat and vulnerability contexts already exist), and an explicit list of what cannot be expressed; (4) the check sheet: what the compiled card opens, responds and overcalls on 40 fixed hands, as a page the card's owner can confirm or correct; (5) declared style as the prior and observed boards as the correction: the off-card rates per family (bid.<family>, bid.miss.<rule>.<feature>) already learn how a player departs from its card.

## Done when

On a branch, PR to feature/gen9: the PDF form reader with a test on a public ACBL card; the agent procedure in docs (with the template) and three cards ingested through it (one photo, one older ACBL, one WBF), each with provenance and check sheet; the style section with at least the preempt and overcall statements Max named compiled and tested on hands; a short report of fields that the cards held and the descriptor could not.

## Context

bridge_harness/cards/card.py (descriptor, defaults, unsupported()), system.py (compiler), docs/gen9/bidding.md section 1 (real cards; intake by an agent then a check sheet). Sample cards: ask Max for his own partnership's card and any he wants modelled; public examples (the ACBL SAYC card, WBF system cards published for world championships) if WebFetch can read them; the sandbox cannot download from most sites.
