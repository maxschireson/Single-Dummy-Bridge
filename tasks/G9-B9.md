Synthetic players with known systems and styles for the arena: a generator that turns a card descriptor (G9-B8) plus style statements into rule-bidder settings and a player package (`kind: synthetic`, declaration filled), so that every system family and style axis in docs/gen9/arena-selfplay.md section 3.1 can have a member.
