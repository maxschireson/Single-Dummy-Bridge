## Goal

Take three published convention cards in different formats (for example the ACBL Standard American Yellow Card, a filled ACBL 2/1 card, one WBF card) and read each into card IR by the procedure a future intake agent would follow. For each: the IR, the provenance block (confidence per field, what could not be placed), and a check sheet showing what the compiled rules open and respond on a fixed set of 40 hands.

## Done when

Three files under `configs/cards/` with check sheets under `reports/`, and a half-page procedure for an intake agent. The field list (G9-B1) is updated with what these cards needed.

## Context

Cite each card's source. Do not copy a card's text into the repository beyond the facts needed for the fields.
