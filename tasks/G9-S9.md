## Goal

The bound of docs/gen9/sid-integration.md section 2.4: how often perfect knowledge of one partner card would let the defence avoid handing declarer a guaranteed line, which is what partner's signals could unlock for defenders' conditional lines.

## Done when

On recorded defender decisions with 8 cards or fewer (Gen 8 feeds), 8X's hand-over odds split by partner's holding of each top or middle run (the cells' classes); the count and share of positions where some card leaves declarer without a guaranteed line in the cells where partner holds the run and not otherwise; by contract class; a short report.
