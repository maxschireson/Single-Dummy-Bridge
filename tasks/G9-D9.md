## Goal

Max (13 Nov 04:06 UTC): 'I like the joint fit strength. We also have priors from bench etc. Can you build that ladder - maybe a timeline that shows the introduction date of a bot vs strength, with a per decision time displayed by each bot? Add it at the top of the arena page.' DATA (dave): scripts/arena_ladder.py writes arena/ladder.json. Players: every bot with boards on the registered feeds (gh-pages arena/gen6-sources.json: Gen 1, 5, 5RZ, 6, 7, 7.1, 8 and its variants, BEN 16 and 64). Strength: your ratings-v1 joint fit (IMPs a board, weighted least squares on pair means, pair effects) as a Bayesian fit: each bot gets a prior, a bench or match estimate relative to its parent where one exists (prior mean = parent + estimate; SD = the estimate's standard error with 10% on the IMPs-per-trick conversion, 3.8 IMPs a trick from E9-0), otherwise a weak prior (SD 2 IMPs) at the field mean; anchor BEN 64 at 0 (an outside bot that does not change) unless you see a better anchor, and say which. Per bot: posterior mean, 95% interval, boards, share of the information that comes from the prior, introduction date (first board on any registered feed), and mean CPU seconds per decision with a choice (from the same records; reuse the source behind the page's decision-timing.js if it has it; say the source). Optional and marked as such: candidates not yet in the arena (9a-32 from the arrival run, 8X from x8's transfer results) as separate points. PAGE (will, with dave's file): arena/ladder.js renders an SVG at the top of arena/index.html: x = introduction date, y = strength with its 95% interval, each point labelled 'name, s per decision', coloured by generation, hollow where the prior carries more than half the information, a tooltip with boards and interval, and a table under the chart. Only two lines change in index.html (a section before the first one, one script tag); everything else is new files.
