## Goal

1. `card_distance(card, generator, deals)`: the rate at which a profile's own bidder breaks the card's rules, over the calls the card covers (architecture 3).
2. `detect(boards, profiles)`: probability over profiles from a pair's finished boards, by the likelihood of their calls on their actual hands under each profile's scorer, with `unknown` as one candidate.
3. E9-2: boards needed to identify the system, for a pair of very different systems, the two house bidders (they differ on about a quarter of deals), and two cards that differ only in the no-trump range.
