## Goal

Design the card-play half of the player model. Five things:

1. **Situations and signals.** A classifier from public information (opening lead, following to partner's lead, following to declarer's lead, first discard, giving a ruff, trump plays) to the signal that applies under a carding descriptor, and for each signal the fact about the hand it states and the spot it prescribes. Lead conventions (fourth best, third and fifth, honour leads) included: they are the most reliable signals.
2. **The class rule**: which of a seat's cards count as "the same play" so that the choice among them is the signal (v1: spots nine and below in the suit played) and how E9-3 tests it.
3. **Carding bots**: synthetic opponents that follow a declared carding scheme with a set honesty, built on the house bot. How they relate to the search's set of exactly tied cards.
4. **The reader term** (architecture 4.3): the formula, vectorized over layouts, and where it enters `play_log_likelihood`.
5. **Estimators** from finished boards: honesty per kind of signal (allowing for the number of spots held), the play factor `phi`, their priors and shrinkage.

And how E9-3 and E9-4 will be measured.
