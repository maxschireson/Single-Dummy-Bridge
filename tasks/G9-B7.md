## Goal

The reader behind the shell's replay_reader hook (bridge_harness/gen9.py): a factory f(model, other, root) returning an object whose type_log_score(length, hon, seat, dealer, vulnerability, auction, profile=<system id>) is the log-likelihood of the seat's calls under that profile's own bidder: the softmax of a BEN-format net (house bidders, BEN's own bidder), the tables of a rule bidder, or a compiled card's rules; None for a profile whose bidder it does not hold. Bidders are found through the Gen 8 system registry (configs/systems/*.json generators) and through a card's generator block. Cached by hand type within a board. Then bot entries: 9r-32 (8p-32 plus replay of the house bidder for the common bidder and for each side's own), 9c-32 and 9x-32 for E9-12.

## Done when

A PR into feature/gen9 (reviewer sam) with tests: on house Gen 6 auctions the true hand's replay score equals the bidder's own softmax product; the pool-level numbers of E9-8 are reproduced through the shell's replay_correction on real positions; no replay_reader key gives the Gen 8 player card for card; seconds per board stated. A 200-board sandbox pilot of 9r-32 against 8p-32 through scripts/gen9_match.py, logged under E9-11.

## Context

E9-8 result; tests/test_gen9_player.py ReplayTests shows the hook with a stand-in reader. The shell applies the reader on the pool after the opening lead only (up to 1,024 rows, one or two hidden seats). Capsule for local matches: commit 8833c62f of arena-gen6-gen7-capture-feed (recovery/manifest.json, artifacts/, assets/).
