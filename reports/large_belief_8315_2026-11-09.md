trajectories enter gradients. The incomplete old mixed block is excluded.

The parent is the currently promoted 308,726-parameter large epoch6 net,
SHA256 `bb46241ed4270ac171354ca9b7d5146bf532f50fc08370c5a5ee07461bbceedb`.

fork audit collection remain owned by their separate task.
