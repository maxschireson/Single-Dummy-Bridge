# E9-17: the convention-card reader against fixed carding rules (t-high, t-low, t-random)

dave, 12 to 13 Nov 2026. Sandbox only (2 CPUs), 3,600 boards, as registered. The source is `feature/gen9-e9-17`: sam's signals-v2 merged with feature/gen9. The bots are in `configs/gen9-e9-17-bots.json`, generated by `scripts/gen9_e9_17_bots.py`.

## What was compared

- **`r9t-32`** is `8x-32` with Gen 9's signal reader. Each stand-in has a carding block over the four first-round spot situations (partner's lead, declarer's lead, first discard, trump):
  - t-high: an emission table that puts the card at the top of its class whatever the message;
  - t-low: the same at the bottom;
  - t-random: no signal in any situation, so an even choice in the class.

  The class itself is read by the net at 8x-32's factor for the type: 0.2, 0.5 or 0.25. Elsewhere, a defender's spot is read as an even choice in its class (sam, 17:22 UTC); the auction is read exactly as 8x-32 reads it (checked).
- **`8x-32`** reads the card itself by the net, at those factors.
- **`8p-32`** reads it by the net at full weight.

The deals are the same for every match (`gen8_match` deals, seed 917, indices 0 to 399). All 400 deals reached the same contract in every pair, so common equals native.

## Against the predictions

- **t-high and t-low, the rule reader beats the scaled net by +0.1 or more:** not shown. t-high's -0.098 (0.142) is below the prediction. t-low's +0.085 (0.141) is within one SE of both +0.1 and zero. Pooled, -0.006 (0.100): no effect is measurable at this size, and +0.1 is one SE away, so it is not excluded either.
- **t-random, the rule reader equals the scaled net:** -0.142 (0.140), within about one SE of zero.
- **t-random, both beat `8p-32`:** not shown. `8x-32` minus `8p-32` is +0.005 (0.181).
- **For comparison, g8's figure:** a full-weight reader loses against t-high, -0.36 (0.13) relative to the scaled reader. Here `8x-32` minus `8p-32` against t-high is +0.328 (0.205), in line with it.

## sam's check (class-top rate)

On `r9t-32~t-high`'s records, t-high's spot is the highest of its class in 93% of the four situations' plays (1,278 occasions; `class_top_rate.py`). t-high plays the highest of its exactly tied cards, and the class can be larger than the tie set. The table told the reader 100%, with a 5% even floor. So in about 7% of occasions the true layout was marked down by about log(0.05/n). A table at the measured rate was not tried; it would be a post-hoc variant past the registered cap. The t-low and t-random matches kept no full records, so their rates are not measured.

## Limitations

- **Coverage.** The rule is read only at the four first-round situations; other spot plays are read as an even choice in the class.
- **Sample size.** 400 deals a match gives standard errors of about 0.14 on a paired difference, which cannot tell 0 from +0.1.
- **No bench figure.** No tricks figure from sam's bench stage was computed for these readers.
