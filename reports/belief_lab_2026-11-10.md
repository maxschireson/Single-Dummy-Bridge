# Twenty-four-hour belief-model experiments

Max authorized belief-model research as this machine's priority through 11 November 2026, 04:30 UTC, including recently archived clean runs. The goal is improvement beyond simply increasing the data volume.

The previous residual-attention experiment used one seed and 192 recent validation families. It beat a dense continuation, but neither improved on unchanged dense v5. This experiment retains that deployed model as the reference and does not automatically promote a replacement.

The frozen expanded corpus has 1,205,661 training positions from 94,262 families and 150,189 development positions from 11,850 families across the historical and recent domains. Recent development coverage is 769 families, including 26,653 positions with two uncertain hidden seats. Another 578 families remain reserved until candidate selection. Original-data and expanded-data controls share exactly the same development pool. All original parent training/holdout boundaries were audited; both cross-boundary overlap counts are zero. The regressed v4/4p policy hash is excluded from both sides of added trajectories. Canonical deal families, rather than cards or positions, are the uncertainty unit.

The queued recipes compare dense continuation on original versus added data; lower learning rate; stronger dropout and weight decay; a Brier-score auxiliary term; public hand-count auxiliary loss; legal ownership-swap conditional loss; ordinary versus consequential-card weighting; earlier v3 initialization; auction-only initialization with zero, one, or three dense pretraining epochs before attention; joint training from random initialization; and wider dense training. Matching dense continuations accompany the staged attention arms. The data-added control and two promising recipes receive two additional seeds, including repeated early pretraining when applicable. Final calibration uses the seed closest to each recipe mean, rather than its luckiest seed; incomplete replications must be disclosed.

An implementation finding changes how the results should be interpreted. `WeightedSampler` uses exact dynamic programming to condition the product of ownership weights on known cards, voids and hand capacities. Multiplying every card's weight for a seat by a constant cancels after conditioning on that seat's fixed count. Iterative capacity projection can therefore improve an unconditioned marginal score without changing the legal-deal distribution. Projection is diagnostic only and cannot be counted as a playing improvement. Temperature can change the distribution and is tested separately.

The legal ownership-swap loss compares the true owners of two hidden cards against swapping those owners, when both assignments obey the public support. It uses hidden ownership only as supervision and adds no hidden information to model inputs. The loss is invariant to per-card and per-seat additive logit offsets, as is the exact sampler. Mixed marginal/swap and pure swap objectives are tested. This is a conditional training experiment, not an assumption that pairwise loss must improve full-game performance.

Epoch selection uses the existing four-domain composite NLL. Final development ranking uses exact two-hidden-seat conditional likelihood, with a composite-NLL guardrail below 0.88, and reports ordinary and weighted card metrics alongside it. Exact likelihood was checked against enumerated legal worlds and for invariance to additive card/seat offsets. Development-selected results are exploratory; seed dispersion is reported separately from family-clustered standard errors.

The queue reserves up to eight hours for screening, up to fourteen total for screening and replication, then freezes its candidate before reserved-family scoring and fresh games. The target is 2,000 paired boards against the data-added dense control and another 2,000 against current v5, using the same deals, promoted v5 policy, candidate-2 v5 bidder, 32 sampled worlds, and swapped partnerships. Common/native duplicate regimes count once. Every fresh confirmation family is evaluation-only. If the deadline interrupts collection, the actual partial count and interval must be reported as such; no result-based stopping or post-confirmation retuning is permitted.

`runs/belief-lab-20261110/plan.json` is the executable plan. `status.json`, `pipeline.log`, per-fit optimizer/RNG checkpoints, `leaderboard.json`, and `progress.md` record progress. A single supervisor lock prevents duplicate queues. The hourly recovery task restarts stopped work from its last atomic checkpoint. Earlier component collection is temporarily drained through the research deadline and its previous authorization is preserved. Continuous uptime is not guaranteed by this environment, so interruptions and active compute must be reported honestly.

Initial validation: public-support reconstruction matches stored masks across all source types; four constraint/likelihood tests and two residual-attention export/resumption tests passed; dense, attention and swap training smoke checks completed. A separate identical-model paired-play check verifies integration and scoring. Results from two-board integration checks are not strength estimates.
