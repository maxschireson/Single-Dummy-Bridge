## Adding a report

Use a dated filename or dated experiment folder. Include the question, exact
source/models/data, method, results, uncertainty, limitations and reproduction/
artifact links. Link the report here or from the relevant subsystem guide.
Distinguish prediction validation, double-dummy proxy evaluations and actual
paired cardplay. Preserve negative results and separate reused families from new
independent evidence.
