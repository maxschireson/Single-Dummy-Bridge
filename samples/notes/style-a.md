## General rules

- Open most balanced hands non-vul with 12 HCP, but could pass a bad one vul.  Open balanced 11 usually only NV and only if especially good (aces, tens).  Unbalanced hands loosely follow the rule of 20, but use judgment based on honor location, spots, , vul, and likely rebid problems.  If you have (say) stiff Q, don’t count that for both 2 HCP and distribution!.  Might pass borderline hands if judgment is that they’ll be easier to describe later.     3rd seat can open light, especially if you want that lead
- Frequently respond light to opening bid, esp non vul

## 1NT opening: range and shape

- Routinely open 1N with 5-card major.  6-card m is possible, but never ‘too good’ a hand.

## Weak two-bids

- 2-level opening should be 6-card suit in 1st/2nd.
- “Good suit” = 2 of top 3.  “Good hand” = 8-10.
- Fourth seat 2-level opening shows a maximum weak 2 bid - definitely less than a solid 1-level opening

## Three- and four-level openings

- 3-level opening should be a 7-card suit with 4-6 playing tricks if non-vul, and 6-7 playing tricks if vul.
- At imps, we play 1st seat FAVORABLE preempt can be VERY light – almost any hand with a 6-card suit would ‘qualify’.  At matchpoints, we do NOT do this, as it rates to lead to a few big gains and more than a few small losses.
- Do not preempt if your hand is too good (even if partner is a PH) or has too many ‘flaws’, such as good playability in side majors (especially spades) or too much stuff in your short suits.  Ideally, you should really want the lead in the suit you preempt in.
- Gambling 3NT: no outside A or K if 1st-3rd seat.

## Notrump overcalls and ranges

- 1N overcall is a good 15-18 HCP.
- Fourth seat (sandwich) NT is 15-18 HCP.
- Balancing 1N is 11-14 vs. 1m; 11-16 vs. 1M.
- Balancing JUMP 2N overcall is 19-21 HCP.
- Double then 1N (or non-jump to 2N) by doubler is 18+-20 HCP
- Double then jump to 2N by doubler is 21-22 HCP
- Balancing double then 1N (or non-jump to 2N) is 15-18 HCP (17-18 after 1M)

## Suit overcalls

- Overcalls are wide range – 7-17 HCP.

## Jump overcalls

- Balancing jump overcall is INTERMEDIATE – about an opening bid with 6-card suit

## They open at the 2-level: take-out doubles

- If advancer bids a suit at the 3-level, this shows values (about 7-10 HCP)
- Balancing double can be a king light

## They open at the 3-level

- Double is Take-out.  Advancer should bid game with 9+ HCP
- Balancing double can be a king light
- Double tends to be for T.O.  Advancer usually needs a six-card suit or 5-5 to go to the 5-level
