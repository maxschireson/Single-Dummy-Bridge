1. t-high / t-low: yes. Rows are indexed by the count message (0 even, 1 odd), the keys match (partner_lead.count, declarer_lead.count, first_discard.count, trump.count), and identical rows make the count carry nothing. The reader mixes in 5% of an even choice (reader_eps), so a card off the rule costs log(0.05 / n), not minus infinity.
2. t-random: [1/3, 1/3, 1/3] is even only for classes of two and three spots; with four or more the middle share is split among n - 2 cards (each 1/6 at four, against 1/3 for lowest and highest). For an even choice at every size, declare the situations with no signal: 'signal_on_partner_lead': 'none', 'signal_on_declarer_lead': 'none', 'first_discard': 'none', 'trump_signal': 'none', and no emission block. SignalModel.signal then returns 'none' and probabilities gives 1/|K|; the block still counts as declared (SignalModel is truthy), so the class term is used.
3. What the block leaves out: only the four first-round situations are read by the rule. Other spot plays (second and later rounds of a suit, discards after the first, every lead after the opening lead) are read as an even choice inside the class; the opening lead by the net (no lead fields), as you say. If t-high's rule applies to all its spot plays, the reader uses it only at those four situations. If you want it everywhere, bridge_harness/signal_expert.py's occasions cover second rounds too (keys partner_lead.r2, declarer_lead.r2, trump.r2), and gen9_reading_bench's ExpertReading shows how a model is applied at them; the SignalPlayer reader itself has no default for un-keyed plays.
4. One check worth making before reading the result: t-high plays the highest of its exactly tied cards, and E9-3's class is the spots nine and below of the suit held, which can be larger than the tie set. So P(highest of the class) for t-high is below 1; if it is well below (0.8, say), [0, 1, 0] will overstate what the high card says. The measured rate from a few hundred of its recorded spot plays would settle it (gen9_house_carding.py does this for the house bots).
