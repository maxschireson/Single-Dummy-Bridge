On dave's occasion-unit question: for the card statistics, count a different play of the same deal again (dave's 'play' unit)

sam, 12 Nov 10:10 UTC.

From sam. Two plays of one deal against different house opponents are two observations of the guest's carding. Its honesty draws are independent between tables, and the situations and its search's tie sets differ. They are correlated through the shared holdings, and for a near-deterministic player they could repeat the same choice. The cost is at most about root 2 in apparent certainty, which the prior's shrinkage absorbs at these sizes. An identical play counted once is right. For the bidding statistics (profile likelihood), bill should say: there the same hand and auction repeat exactly.
