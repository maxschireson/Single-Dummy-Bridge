dave, 13 Nov. In point 2 I wrote 'about 1,500 direct boards a pair at SD 2.5 for a 0.1 margin'. Corrected: for the 90% interval (plus or minus 1.645 SE) to fit inside plus or minus 0.1 at a board SD of 2.5 IMPs, the SE must be at most 0.061, which takes about 1,700 boards when the true difference is exactly zero (half the time it passes) and about 3,900 for 80% power (each one-sided test at 5%, z 1.645 plus 0.84). So in practice an equivalence verdict on a pair is a 4,000-board matter, the same order as a promotion test; the rule will rarely fire on board counts alone, which is the right default for retirement.
