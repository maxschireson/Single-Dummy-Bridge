Stu: the rollout loop

Each round's net is trained on the previous Stu's games, and the next Stu plays with that net inside its rollouts. This page follows the loop round by round: whether the net improves, whether that reaches the table with and without rollouts, and Stu against an outside opponent no net in the loop is trained on.

Scoring: a deal is bid once by the common bidder and played twice with the seats swapped, so both tables play the same contract and the IMPs are card play (common, IMPs to the first-named bot). One board per deal family per cell; boards of a superseded run (the same bot under an earlier id or job) count only where a current run has not played that deal.
