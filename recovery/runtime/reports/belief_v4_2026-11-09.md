# Belief v4: additive update, 2026-11-09

The 308,726-parameter network continues the deployed large belief and its Adam state. Three passes retain historical replay and give recent deal families twice the total mass; extra card positions and forks do not multiply a family's weight. Epoch 3 was selected. This checkpoint has not yet been deployed or measured for IMP strength.

Training used 126,608 distinct families, 1,630,369 positions and 47,247,357 uncertain-card targets per pass. These are 141,742,071 target presentations over three passes, not independent observations. All original train/validation/test assignments were retained. The 49,009 incoming/local auction families include both local and remote corpora, deduplicated by exact auction transcript.

These are paired, deal-family-clustered descriptive intervals on selection validation, not an untouched final test. Aggregate covariance keeps families shared across auction/play domains together (20,837 distinct families). No independent-card assumption is used. Nominal aggregate NLL p is 1.20e-8, accuracy p=0.107. A three-epoch selection adjustment alone would not change the NLL conclusion, but repeated use of validation prevents a clean confirmatory interpretation.

Auction-only completed-auction NLL worsened: old BEN +0.0002925 (5.33 SE), old DDS auctions +0.0002786 (4.54 SE), recent bidder +0.0001900 (4.76 SE), recent BEN +0.0001619 (0.85 SE). The improvement is a trade toward public-play prediction, not uniform progress.

The new fit has three epochs versus the previous eight-pass size study and starts from an already improved model.

The normal arena, including the retained predecessor, supplies subsequent playing-strength evidence. No special experimental promotion gate is introduced.
